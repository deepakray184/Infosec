QUICK ANSWERS — BEFORE WE TALK

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Still deciding where to start? Here are a few things teams and students commonly ask.

What does a managed SOC engagement cover?

Coverage can include monitoring and alert triage, investigation, threat hunting, incident coordination, reporting, and ongoing detection improvement. The data sources, hours, response authority, and service levels are agreed during scoping.

Can you work with our existing security tools and team?

Yes. We start by understanding your current tools, workflows, and team responsibilities, then agree the integrations and handoffs that make sense for your environment.

How is a VAPT engagement scoped?

We define the assets, testing window, rules of engagement, and points of contact before testing begins. Findings are documented with impact and remediation guidance; retesting can be included by agreement.

How does student cybersecurity training work?

Training can be tailored around SOC analyst foundations or ethical VAPT, with guided practice in controlled labs. Cohort size, prerequisites, schedule, and delivery format are discussed before a program is confirmed.
